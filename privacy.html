Overview

Memory Flip Master is designed as a casual memory game. This policy explains how the app may handle information when you play the game.

Information We Process

The game may use device identifiers such as IDFV for security, anti-abuse, fraud prevention, and service stability. If advertising services are enabled, the app may request App Tracking Transparency permission before initializing supported advertising SDKs.

Advertising and Third-Party SDKs

The app may integrate security and advertising SDKs, including Chartboost and Yandex Mobile Ads. Advertising SDKs are initialized only after remote ad configuration is available and the ATT request flow has completed.

Data Use

Information may be used to protect the app, prevent abuse, maintain stability, serve rewarded ads when available, and improve the gameplay experience. The game does not require account registration.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.
